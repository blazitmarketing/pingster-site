Delete your Pingster account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Pingster and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account forever.

Without the app

Email support@pingsterapp.com from the email address you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 7 days and let you know when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email address and username
  • All your stories, photos and videos
  • Your comments, likes and saved stories

Deleting your account can't be undone.

Copies in Pingster's safety backups disappear on their own within 7 days. If an account was banned for breaking the community rules, its email address stays on the banned list.

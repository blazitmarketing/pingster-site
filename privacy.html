Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Pingster is a free app for sharing photos and short videos of places, pinned to the spot on the map where they were taken. This page explains, in plain words, what information Pingster keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

  • We only keep what the app needs to work.
  • We never sell your information.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

What Pingster keeps

  • Your account: your email address, your username and your password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, including us).
  • What you share: your photos and videos, the spot on the map where you pinned them, the place name, your description and hashtags, and when you posted.
  • What you do in the app: your comments, likes, saved stories, the people you block, and reports you send about posts or comments.
  • Safety records: if a post or account is reported or removed for breaking the community rules, we keep a note of what happened. If an account is banned, its email address stays on a banned list so it can't sign up again.

Your location

Pingster asks to use your phone's location so it can show stories near you and pin new stories to the right spot. Your location is used on your phone for this and is not stored or tracked. The only locations Pingster keeps are the spots of the stories you choose to share, and those are shown to everyone on the map.

Your photos and videos

Before a photo is shared, Pingster removes the hidden information cameras add to pictures (such as camera details). Every new story is checked by Pingster before other people can see it. Once a story is approved, anyone using Pingster can see it, along with your username.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: show stories on the map, let you post, comment, like and save.
  • To send account emails, like confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • To keep Pingster safe: review new stories, handle reports, and stop people who break the rules.

We don't send marketing emails, and we don't use your information to build advertising profiles.

Who helps us run Pingster

Pingster uses a few trusted services that store or handle information only to run the app:

  • Supabase: stores accounts, stories, photos and videos (servers in the United States).
  • Resend: sends Pingster's account emails.
  • Apple Maps and Google Maps: show the maps inside the app, and directions when you tap "Take me there".
  • Photon (by Komoot), using OpenStreetMap data: when you type a place for an event, the words you type and your rough area (about a mile) are sent to find matching places. Nothing about you is sent.

Place and town names come from OpenStreetMap: map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (openstreetmap.org/copyright).

If Pingster starts showing ads in the future, we'll update this policy first and explain exactly what changes.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your stories, photos, videos, comments, likes and saved stories are deleted right away. Copies in our safety backups disappear on their own within 7 days. Banned email addresses and safety records stay so the rules can be enforced.

Your choices

  • Delete a story: open it and tap "Delete story".
  • Delete your whole account: Profile, then "Delete my account" at the bottom. More about deleting your account.
  • Block someone: open one of their stories and tap "Block".
  • Location: you can turn off location for Pingster in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Questions or requests: email support@pingsterapp.com.

Children

Pingster is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Pingster's information is protected with locked-down access rules, encrypted connections and daily backups. No system is perfect, but we work hard to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, let you know in the app.

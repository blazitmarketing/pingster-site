Help & support

Need a hand? Email support@pingsterapp.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

Common questions

I didn't get my confirmation email.
Check your spam or junk folder for an email from Pingster. Still nothing? Try signing up again in a few minutes, or email us.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. We'll send a link to choose a new one.

Why isn't my story showing on the map yet?
Every new story is checked before other people can see it. You'll find it in your Profile marked "Waiting for approval" until then.

Someone posted something that breaks the rules.
Open the story and tap "Report story", or tap the flag next to a comment. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also block that person.

How do I delete a story?
Open your story and tap "Delete story" at the bottom.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile, then "Delete my account" at the bottom. More details.
